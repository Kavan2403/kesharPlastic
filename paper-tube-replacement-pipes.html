Buyer questions

Before you enquire

Can you make cores to our diameter and length?

Yes. We manufacture based on the business requirement and order. Share the inside and outside diameters, wall requirement, length, quantity and application so feasibility can be discussed.

Do you supply tubes for insulator export packaging?

Protective packaging for insulators is an application to discuss with us. Share component dimensions, clearance, cushioning, closure and transport requirements. These tubes are packaging components, not electrical insulators; suitability depends on the complete packing design.

Can you replace the paper cores used on our line?

Send the existing core measurements, machine-fit requirements and the problem you want to address. The replacement specification and suitability need to be discussed for your process.

What are your minimum order and lead time?

Quantity, specification and delivery requirements are discussed with the quotation. No fixed minimum order or production lead time is published here.
