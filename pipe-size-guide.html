Measuring for a plastic core

Inside diameter / bore

Measure the opening that must fit the winding shaft, chuck or the item packed inside the tube. State which measurement you are providing.

Outside diameter & wall

Include the outside diameter and any wall-thickness requirement. Do not assume a trade size identifies the complete core specification.

Length & quantity

Give the finished core length, units and order quantity. Discuss any cut-length tolerance that matters to your process.

Application & handling

Describe the fabric, tarpaulin, roll or packed product. Include handling conditions and any machine or load requirements.
