What happens to your information

Enquiry details

When you click Send enquiry by email, the form sends your name, phone number, selected product or service, requirement and any optional details you provide to our Cloudflare enquiry service. It validates the request and uses Hostinger to send it to our business inbox at info@shreekesharplastic.com. We use these details to respond to and manage your enquiry. Preparing a WhatsApp message remains a separate, optional action. The website does not create a separate customer database or enrol you in advertising lists.

Enquiry emails may remain in the business mailbox and its backups until removed by the business or provider. Contact us to discuss access, correction or deletion. Please avoid sensitive information in the form.

WhatsApp, calls and email links

Preparing a WhatsApp message happens in your browser. It is shared with WhatsApp only when you choose to open WhatsApp; you then choose whether to send it there. Call and email links open your chosen app. These services have their own privacy practices.

Optional analytics

With your permission, Google Analytics measures page visits and actions such as call clicks and WhatsApp handoffs. We do not send your form name, phone, email address or requirement text as analytics event parameters. A click is not proof of a completed call or sale.

Analytics is off until you allow it. Your choice is stored in this browser for up to six months. You can change it using the Privacy choices button in the footer. Rejecting analytics does not prevent an enquiry. Withdrawing permission stops future analytics on this site; it does not erase earlier data already processed by Google.

Advertising

No Meta Pixel or Google Ads remarketing tag is enabled in this version. Advertising consent remains off. If advertising tracking is introduced, this notice and the available consent choices must be updated first.

Security and supporting services

GitHub Pages serves this public website and processes network information to deliver its pages. Cloudflare processes form submissions and network information, including your IP address for abuse prevention. Hostinger processes enquiry emails and stores them in our business mailbox and sent folder. Application code does not log your form fields or credentials. Provider security and operational records may still apply. No automatic reply is sent to visitors. Google Fonts supplies the typefaces; YouTube provides the external trencher thumbnail and video link. Those services receive network information when their resources are loaded. They are separate from optional analytics.
