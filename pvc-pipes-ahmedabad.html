Bring the route and the specification together.

Ordering for an existing line

Read the pipe markings and measure the connection before ordering. A familiar inch name alone may not identify the required product.

  • Size and markings on the existing pipe
  • Pressure class or project specification
  • Socket, fitting and joining requirements

Planning a new route

Share the route length, intended use and water-supply details if it is an irrigation project. Confirm the pipe specification before deciding the quantity.

  • Site location and planned route
  • Required lengths and fitting schedule
  • Supply-only or installation requirement

What to include in a PVC supply enquiry

Send the size, required class or standard if specified by your project, length, quantity and delivery location. If a particular brand is required, name it in the enquiry. We confirm the available product and terms rather than treating every size as permanently in stock.
