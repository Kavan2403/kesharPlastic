Buyer resources / Installation planning

Prepare your farm for the installation discussion.

Our complete service covers pipe supply, trenching, laying, jointing, testing and backfilling within 100 km of Ahmedabad. This checklist helps you prepare the enquiry; the detailed site sequence is agreed for the project.

Get a quote WhatsApp Call now

Before a quotation

Send the location

Share the farm or village location so we can discuss the 100 km service radius, approach route and site access.

Explain the route

Give the approximate length, start and end points, branches, known obstacles and any existing buried services. Do not begin excavation based only on an online guide.

Discuss material & scope

Share the pipe specification if already decided. Confirm the complete scope, site constraints and project-specific terms with the quotation.

Agree the site arrangements

Discuss access, a contact person at the farm, material handling and the required testing arrangements before scheduling the work.

Work included in the service

The included activities are confirmed. Depth, testing method and execution details depend on the site and agreed requirement.

Pipe supply

Discuss the pipe specification and quantity as part of the installation requirement.

Trenching

Trencher-based work along the discussed farm pipeline route.

Laying

Positioning the pipeline along the prepared route.

Jointing

Connecting the selected pipe system as part of the installation.

Testing

Testing is included. The method and project requirements are discussed with the quotation.

Backfilling

Backfilling forms part of the complete service, not a separate task left to the farmer.
