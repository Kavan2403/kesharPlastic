PVC vs HDPE: compare the complete pipe requirement.

A material name alone is not enough to choose an irrigation pipeline. Compare the proposed specification, route, connections and installation method. Shree Keshar Plastic supplies both PVC and HDPE.

Get a quote WhatsApp Call now

Ask these questions before choosing.

What will the line do?

Describe the water source, required delivery and whether the pipe is part of a new layout or an existing system. Give the operating requirement rather than choosing by colour.

How will it follow the route?

Mark bends, access restrictions and changes in level. Confirm the supplied pipe format, permitted bending or fitting requirements for the actual product being quoted.

How will the joints be made?

Compare compatible fittings, connections and the equipment needed for the proposed pipe system. Material cost alone does not cover the whole installation.

Which specification must it meet?

State pressure class, grade, standard or project requirements where known. Ask for the offered product details and supporting documents before ordering.
