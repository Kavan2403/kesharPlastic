An industrial enquiry starts with fit.

Machine or process compatibility

Send the required outside diameter, length and wall requirement alongside the inch trade size. Include a drawing or mating-part measurements if fit is critical.

  • Explain what the pipe will carry or support
  • Identify any clearance or connection requirement
  • Agree suitability before placing the order

Recurring procurement

For a repeat requirement, share the quantity per order and the specification you need to maintain. Discuss tolerances and delivery requirements rather than relying only on a previous size label.

  • Trade size and measured dimensions
  • Length and quantity per order
  • Delivery location and required timing

Choosing by colour is not enough.

Black describes appearance, not a guaranteed material grade or pressure rating. If the requirement involves water pressure, chemicals, load or a particular standard, tell us before ordering. We will discuss the product specification needed for that application.

Buyer questions

Before you enquire

Are these inside-diameter sizes?

No. Our black pipes are measured by outside diameter (OD), with trade sizes expressed in inches. Ask for the exact dimensional specification when fit or tolerances are important.

Which sizes come in continuous coils?

The 2, 3 and 4-inch trade sizes are supplied as continuous coils, with a maximum length of 100 metres. Confirm the length you need when requesting a quote.

Do the listed lengths mean a minimum order?

No. They are maximum supplied lengths. Quantity, required length, supplied format and availability are confirmed for the order.
